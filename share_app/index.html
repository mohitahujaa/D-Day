Set up

Open the setup link from the D-Day dashboard on your PC (Settings → Share from phone → scan the QR code).

On: copy a message (e.g. in WhatsApp), then open D-Day. The copied text is sent automatically.

✓

Sent to D-Day

Your PC will pick it up within a minute (when it's on) and show it in the D-Day inbox.

How to use
  1. Install: in Chrome's ⋮ menu choose Add to Home screen / Install app.
  2. In SMS, Gmail and most apps: long-press or select text → Share → D-Day → Send.
  3. WhatsApp text messages can't be shared to other apps: turn on Copy mode, copy the message, then open D-Day. It sends the copied text by itself.
  4. It goes to your "D-Day" list in Google Tasks; your PC reads it with its local model. Nothing is added to your calendar until you confirm on the PC.

Google sign-in is used only to add the message to your own Google Tasks.
